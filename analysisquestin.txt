Recommendations

Overall Business Conclusion

The statistical analysis suggests that the current ride pricing system shows weak relationships among distance, fare, surge pricing, and customer ratings. Most hypothesis tests failed to reject the null hypothesis (p > 0.05), indicating that the observed differences are likely due to random variation. Therefore, the company should investigate additional factors such as route, traffic, customer preferences, and location-based demand to improve pricing and operational efficiency.
